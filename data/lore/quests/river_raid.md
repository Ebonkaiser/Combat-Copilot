# Quest: The River Raid — COMPLETED

**Assigned by:** Zhu, described as the most time-critical of three new missions.
**Objective:** Retake a hijacked Imperial supply barge from the Sun-Eater Sect near Gak Lei-Kun before they detonate its black-powder cargo and kill the hostages aboard.

**Outcome:** Party disguised themselves as Sun-Eater Sect members and boarded the barge. Cleared the barracks (2 berserkers), freed ~10 hostages from the lunchroom, and defeated **Lieutenant Jin the Scorched** and his crew on the bridge despite blinding powder and black-powder blasts. Iron/Duke Horn transformed into "Iron" mid-fight and, post-battle, panicked off the ship before Toru calmed him with hay. Rose and U.G. piloted the reclaimed barge back to Gak Lei-Kun with its cargo and rescued hostages.
