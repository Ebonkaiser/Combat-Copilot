# Quest: The Stolen Meridian Scroll — INVESTIGATION COMPLETED, SCROLLS RECOVERED

**Assigned by:** Zhu, the Empress's liaison.
**Objective:** Investigate a locked-room murder at Gak Lei-Kun (Bureau inspector Vahn, chest caved by a single palm strike, safe emptied of the Meridian Scrolls) and recover the stolen document before it triggers a factional civil war.

**Investigation findings:** No forced entry; window lock picked with rare silk wire (Cloud-Tread Syndicate method); killing-blow bruise shaped like a blooming orchid, later confirmed via checks to match Ghost-Mountain iron-body conditioning rather than the Jade Orchid Academy. A sulfur-scented Draconic note on the body implicated the escaped mage **Adovarius**.

**Recovery:** The scrolls were later recovered near Tian Wei — dropped by Adovarius's acolyte **Shao the Unbroken** when Rose's crossbow knocked him from the bamboo (see Catch Your Ass session). The party never returned them to Zhu/the Bureau; Adovarius subsequently repurposed them as a rebel counterintelligence weapon after the Sanctuary of Starlight defection.
