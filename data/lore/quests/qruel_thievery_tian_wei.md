# Quest: Cruel Thievery (Tian Wei Investigation) — COMPLETED

**Assigned by:** Zhu, following the party's report after the Ghost-Mountain Monks resolution.
**Objective:** Determine why the village of Tian Wei is dying and its residents' chi is being drained.

**Outcome:** Party descended into an Underdark passage beneath a dry well, fought venomous "danger noodles" and tentacled ambushers, found a betrayal-vision mosaic, and destroyed a **ki-draining locust** at the heart of the complex. The drain lifted instantly; villagers recovered; sun returned to Tian Wei. On the road back, the party also confirmed the Cloud-Tread Syndicate as the source of their silent bounty by killing three of its mercenaries robbing Chinzo.
