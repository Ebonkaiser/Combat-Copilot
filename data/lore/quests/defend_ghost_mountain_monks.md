# Quest: Defend the Ghost Mountain Monks — COMPLETED

**Trigger:** A gaunt messenger delivered a scroll (seal matching Toru's amulet) reading "an old friend awaits," summoning the party to the Ghost-Mountain monastery.

**Objective:** Stop Toru's former orc handler from marching a regiment on the monastery to reclaim him; the Acting Grandmaster made clear the party would be executed if the monks fell.

**Outcome:** Party traveled to the orc camp. Toru surrendered himself; the handler revealed he intended to kill everyone regardless and raid the monks anyway. Toru struck first, triggering the orc rite of ritual combat. Iron/Duke Horn's "Iron" personality emerged for the first time, goring the handler to death — then turned on Toru before being subdued. Toru revived the handler with Hand of Healing and killed him properly with a palm strike. The watching orcs acclaimed **Toru and Iron/Duke Horn as new joint commanders** of the regiment. On the road home, the party also destroyed an undead chi-draining creature. Returned to the monastery: Toru declared an ally, rewards given (5,000 silver each, Ghost-Mountain sash for Toru, +1 short sword for Rose, wagon repaired).
