# Quest: The Jiangshi of the Song Gong Trail — COMPLETED

**Assigned by:** Zu, the Empress's representative.
**Objective:** Eliminate a creature terrorizing caravans along a vital supply line through the bamboo forest.

**Outcome:** The "Jiangshi" was a rogue Ghost-Mountain monk using a forbidden blood-draining technique, accompanied by a grieving albino ape companion. Party defeated him (ape fled, later left in peace after Rose's natural-20 charisma check). Recovered a Ghost-Mountain amulet and sash from his cave hideout, later reunited via a wandering pilgrim. This encounter opened the ongoing Ghost-Mountain Monks storyline (Grandmaster's unresolved assassination, Toru's backstory).
