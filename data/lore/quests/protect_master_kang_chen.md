# Quest: Protect Master Kang Chen — COMPLETED

**Assigned by:** Zhu, following the River Raid, concerning fallout from the original Grand Lotus Tea House sabotage in Ling Hai.
**Objective:** Master Kang Chen has been poisoned twice and is dying; the Cloud-Tread Syndicate is vowing blood against the Iron Silk Bureau for failing to protect neutral ground. Identify the poisoner and clear the party's name.

**Outcome:** Investigation (Duke's nose, Toru's search) uncovered a hidden laboratory beneath the tea house and its resident, **Hanzo the Bitter Draught**. Captured but evasive, Hanzo threatened Kang Chen's life for 50,000 silver. The party broke his sealed antidote scrolls with Rose's poison, brewed the cure from his own supplies, and administered it in time — Kang Chen survived. A Cloud-Tread vice lieutenant then took Hanzo and the evidence into custody without a confession, leaving the party's full exoneration and the true mastermind unresolved.
