# Quest: The Blood Feud Treaty — COMPLETED

**Assigned by:** Zu, the Empress's representative (post-Adovarius capture).
**Objective:** Sabotage a financial/marital alliance treaty between the Jade Orchid Academy and Cloud-Tread Syndicate at the Grand Lotus Tea House — if the alliance succeeds, their combined influence threatens the Iron Silk Bureau's power.

**Outcome:** Party infiltrated as kitchen/serving staff, poisoned the Syndicate delegation's tea with toxic flowers gathered outside the kitchen window. Syndicate blamed the Academy; swords drawn; treaty permanently collapsed. Party escaped undetected with fleeing staff.

**Consequence:** Sparked the Cloud-Tread Syndicate's ongoing silent bounty against anyone matching the party's description.
