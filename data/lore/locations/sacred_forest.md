# The Sacred Forest

Dense, treacherous bamboo wilderness on the border between the Holy Seat of Milin and the Holy State of Regis. Thick growth, fog, cold dampness, and difficult underbrush make crossing hazardous; functions as a natural barrier preventing open invasion between the two powers.

## Key Event

Following Adovarius's directions north of Tian Wei, the party entered the forest pursuing the scent of a fire and Shao the Unbroken. The chase led through an **ancient magical veil** hidden among frost-dusted bamboo stalks — a threshold completely obscuring the **Sanctuary of Starlight** beyond it.
