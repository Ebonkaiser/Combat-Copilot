# Grand Lotus Tea House

Masterwork pavilion in Zhong Gong: polished dark mahogany suspended over a jade-green lake on stilts, reached by a long bridge; open slatted walls, jasmine/chrysanthemum tea scent, a long indoor koi pond historically used to physically separate rival factions during negotiations, a blind guzheng musician.

## Key Events

- Site of the sabotaged **Blood Feud treaty** between the Jade Orchid Academy and Cloud-Tread Syndicate — the party infiltrated as kitchen/serving staff and poisoned the Syndicate's tea with toxic flowers, collapsing negotiations into open brawling.
- Later: a concealed stairway behind a loose wall-mounted bandolier near the kitchen led to a hidden underground laboratory (luminescent mushrooms, herbs, poison compounds, a lived-in sleeping area) belonging to **Hanzo the Bitter Draught**, discovered while investigating Master Kang Chen's poisoning. Two sealed antidote scrolls (living-and-dead tree sigil) were recovered and used to save Kang Chen. A hidden floor-by-floor map of secret routes through the building was also found.
