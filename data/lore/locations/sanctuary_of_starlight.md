# Sanctuary of Starlight

Remote, ancient temple carved into mountain rock, hidden in the bamboo foothills north of Tian Wei, reached by crossing an ancient magical veil in the Sacred Forest. Sun-bleached marble, aged cedar, raw crystals pulsing with starlight; crumbling murals depict the marriage of the farmer Milin and the crystal dragon Ankamideus, and a ruined altar to the First Empress. Its ambient magical glow openly defies the current Empress's anti-magic decrees. Zhu's broken cipher, once repaired, reveals coordinates leading directly here.

## The Trials

Swinging pendulum blades, a 50-foot animated armored statue (torch + club), a riddle door ("a throne"), a trapped bridge (pressure plates, wind turbines, flame jets, falling hazards), a false wall hiding a cache (greater healing potion), and a "bait" tripwire that drops an explosive cistern on anyone who assumes it's the only danger.

## The True Empress's Offer

At the temple's heart, Adovarius, Shao the Unbroken, and robed acolytes awaited. Adovarius made his case for Ankamideus as the true, wrongfully banished Empress. **U.G. broke his pact with the current Empress and accepted Ankamideus**, and persuaded Iron/Duke Horn and Rose to join. Adovarius healed the party, became their handler, and granted them rest at level 4. In a later session, U.G. was drawn into an astral audience here and formally bound as **Executioner of Her Will**; Iron/Duke Horn swore a parallel blood oath.
