# Gak Lei-Kun

Sprawling river town on the Imperial River; main headquarters of the Iron Silk Bureau. Built as a fortified garrison rather than a living community — jagged black-timber/stone palisades, charcoal-and-crimson Bureau colors, soldiers marching in synchronized formation, iron gallows with bamboo cages holding dissidents lining the main thoroughfare. Dominated by a Bureau citadel on a stone pier over the river rapids, banners bearing Milin's gold solar seal, iron-hulled warships patrolling with chained ballistas.

## Key Events

- **The locked-room murder:** A high-ranking Bureau inspector (Vahn) was found dead, chest caved by a single palm strike, his safe emptied of the Meridian Scrolls. No forced entry — window lock picked with rare silk wire (Cloud-Tread Syndicate method). The killing-blow bruise shape initially suggested Jade Orchid Academy but was confirmed via checks to match Ghost-Mountain iron-body conditioning. A sulfur-scented Draconic note on the body implicated Adovarius.
- Serves as the party's base of operations for the River Raid mission (barge retrieval from the Sun-Eater Sect); the reclaimed barge was returned here successfully.
