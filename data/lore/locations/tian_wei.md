# Tian Wei

Remote town ~400 miles northeast of Gak Lei-Kun, deep in gray-green bamboo forest. Depicted as a dying ghost town: weathered timber cabins, rotting thatch, no children or merchants, hollow silence, sickly gray-skinned residents coughing behind shut doors. The local ki/qi felt sluggish and cold to anyone who approached.

## The Investigation (Resolved)

Iron/Duke Horn's Divine Sense traced the disturbance underground. After questioning a frail old woman (smoothed over with 50 silver after an awkward incident), the party found a long-dry well at the village center and descended into an **Underdark passage**:
- A broken magical circle room (former conjuring space)
- A swarm of venomous "danger noodles" (snakes) — Toru absorbed the worst of it (9 Con saves)
- A buried treasure cache (10,000 silver, diamonds, quartz, 4 Potions of Healing)
- A **mosaic showing each viewer's deepest personal betrayal**
- Tentacled ambushers dropping from the ceiling, and mounting exhaustion (2 levels deep)
- The source: a **ki-draining locust** glowing green on a stone pedestal, actively bleeding qi from the surroundings

The party destroyed the locust. The drain lifted immediately — horn-cap warmth returned, villagers' color returned, and the sun broke through the snow clouds over Tian Wei. **Tian Wei has been healed.**
