# Draxis

Riverside village under Sun-Eater Sect banners (cracked-sun crest); rebels openly mock Imperial law. Home to Chinzo's traveling shop stopovers, Morgan "Pickle" Pickleny's apothecary, gambling, cheap liquor, opium smoke, and black powder in every alley. The party's first front in the rebellion against embedded Imperial spies.

## Key Events

- Every party member passed a DC 15 Deception check to disguise themselves entering town (Iron/Duke Horn's natural 20 hid his horns under an afro).
- Tracked the Imperial spy **Chen Nguyen** ("Master Chen") here, via Pickle's tip and Rose's tail, to a concealed underground apothecary guarded by an umber hulk ("Bug George").
- Completed the party's **first spy-removal operation**: Chen Nguyen poisoned, body hidden in Bug George's remains, tunnel collapsed with black powder.
