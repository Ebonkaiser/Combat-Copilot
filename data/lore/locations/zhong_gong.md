# Zhong Gong (also called Zhongdong)

Small, secluded village in a misty valley; cobblestone paths between dark timber teahouses on a jade-green lake. During tension, villagers lock indoors and armed guards patrol.

Home to the **Grand Lotus Tea House**, central landmark and neutral meeting ground between rival factions. Site of the sabotaged "Blood Feud" treaty signing between the Jade Orchid Academy and Cloud-Tread Syndicate, and later the discovery of Hanzo the Bitter Draught's hidden poison lab beneath the tea house. Also the location where the party regularly reports to Zhu for new mission scrolls.
