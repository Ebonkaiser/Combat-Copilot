# Cloud-Tread Syndicate

**Role:** Wealthy merchant guild and network of information brokers.

**Location:** East, near Kama.

**Martial Style — The Weightless Shadow:** Masters of Qinggong (lightness kung fu). Unmatched agility — run up walls, leap across roofs, fight mid-air using throwing needles and hidden blades.

**Philosophy:** "Gold flows like water; a wise man simply builds the dam."

**The Conflict:** Owns the docks, tea houses, and trade routes. No loyalty to honor or rebellion — only profit. Plays a dangerous double game: selling weapons to Sun-Eater rebels while selling information about those same rebels to the Iron Silk Bureau.

## Campaign History

- After the Blood Feud treaty sabotage, the Syndicate placed a **silent bounty** on anyone matching the description of Grand Lotus Tea House kitchen/serving staff — the party. Bounty hunters have collected on it twice: once for 500 silver/head (party paid rather than fight), and once when three armed retainers ambushed the party near Tian Wei (party killed all three).
- **Master Kang Chen**, a Syndicate leader, was poisoned twice at the Grand Lotus Tea House; the party's investigation implicated Hanzo the Bitter Draught, not the party, though Kang Chen initially suspected them.
- A Cloud-Tread "vice lieutenant" took custody of Hanzo and the poisoned tea leaves after Kang Chen's recovery — his personal involvement felt suspicious and unresolved.
- The Syndicate is suspected (via recovered silk lockpicking wire) in the original Meridian Scroll theft/murder at Gak Lei-Kun, though this may be a frame.
- Two Imperial spies embedded in the Syndicate are named in the Meridian Scrolls as future rebel removal targets (not yet acted on).
