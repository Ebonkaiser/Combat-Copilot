# Ghost-Mountain Monks

**Role:** Faction of ascetic isolationists and guardians of secrets. Party status: **Allied** (as of session "Iron Comes Out to Play").

**Location:** Directly adjacent to the shogunate of Kama; monastery in the foothills near Draxis.

**Martial Style — The Unyielding Mountain:** Heavy, slow, iron-body conditioning — trained by striking iron sand and stones until skin can deflect blades. Fight unarmed or with heavy wooden staves; crushing grapples and devastating palms.

**Philosophy:** "True strength is found in stillness and self-mastery."

**The Conflict:** Guard an ancient repository of martial arts manuals every other clan wants. Historically neutral, but recently closed their mountain passes after their Grandmaster was murdered under unresolved circumstances — they are quietly determining who to crush in retribution.

## A Rogue Element

A member had gone rogue: a nameless outcast monk (per custom, Ghost-Mountain Monks take no names) who kept a baby albino gorilla companion and practiced a forbidden blood-draining technique — the source of the "Jiangshi" legend on the Song Gong Trail. The party defeated him; his cave hideout yielded a coded journal (deep regret over his expulsion) and a sash later completed with his amulet and returned to a wandering pilgrim who mourned him.

The palm-strike murder of the Bureau inspector at Gak Lei-Kun (Meridian Scrolls theft) was later confirmed via medicine/history checks to match Ghost-Mountain iron-body conditioning, not the Jade Orchid Academy as initially suspected — implicating the order (or someone trained in their methods) further.

## The Monastery, Toru, and the Orc Threat

The late Grandmaster was revealed to be the same wandering master who had once beaten and reformed **Toru** (a former orc assassin sent to kill him). The Acting Grandmaster held Toru responsible for a regiment of orcs — led by Toru's old handler — marching to reclaim him, and threatened execution of the whole party if the monastery fell.

The party resolved the threat: Toru and Iron/Duke Horn defeated the orc handler in ritual combat and were acclaimed as the regiment's new joint commanders. The Acting Grandmaster declared Toru an official ally, rewarding the party (5,000 silver each, Toru's Ghost-Mountain sash, Rose's +1 short sword, wagon repairs, poison-crafting herbs).
