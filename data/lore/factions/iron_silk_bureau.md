# Iron Silk Bureau

**Role:** Imperial Lawbringers and Secret Police of the realm.

**Location:** Centrally located, concentrated around the holy Seat. Headquartered at the fortified river town of Gak Lei-Kun.

**Martial Style — The Bound Meridian Stance:** Heavy iron-weighted sashes and chained throwing daggers to grapple, disarm, and pin down faster opponents. Specializes in pressure-point strikes that temporarily paralyze limbs.

**Philosophy:** "Without order, the realm is just a cage of beasts."

**The Conflict:** As official enforcers of the Empress, the Bureau views all other clans as potential rebels. It squeezes the other clans with heavy taxes and weapon bans, using a massive spy network to maintain a chokehold on the region.

## Current Status (post Sanctuary of Starlight)

The party has broken from the Imperial cause after accepting Adovarius's account that Milin bound and banished Ankamideus, and that the current Empress's line suppresses magic to retain stolen power. U.G. formally broke his pact with the current Empress and bound himself to Ankamideus; Rose and Iron/Duke Horn joined the effort to dethrone the current Empress. The party still holds the recovered Meridian Scrolls and Zhu's broken cipher wheel, creating direct conflict between their new allegiance and the Bureau's intelligence interests.

The party has begun actively dismantling the Bureau's spy network:
- The Meridian Scrolls name 12 Imperial spies embedded across the Five Clans.
- Immediate targets: 2 each embedded in the Sun-Eater Sect, Jade Orchid Academy, and Cloud-Tread Syndicate.
- Plan: quietly remove agents and install loyal replacements, seizing intelligence channels for Ankamideus's movement.
- **Chen Nguyen** (Sun-Eater Sect infiltrator) has been eliminated — first successful removal. Coded prescription slips recovered from his body show he transmitted medical logs and rebel identities to the Bureau. His Bureau codebook was also recovered.
- **Master Ba** (Sun-Eater Sect, second target) is under surveillance in Xiaoguanzhou as of the most recent session.
