# Jade Orchid Academy

**Role:** Faction of scholarly aristocrats and traditionalists.

**Location:** North, near the holy state of Regis.

**Martial Style — The Whispering Jian:** Elegant, precise, defensive. Flexible double-edged swords, absolute economy of motion. Believe a fight should be won before a blade is drawn, relying on impeccable footwork to make enemies defeat themselves.

**Philosophy:** "Virtue is the highest blade; tradition is the strongest shield."

**The Conflict:** Consider themselves the moral compass of the realm — incredibly arrogant. Look down on the Sun-Eater Sect as common thugs and distrust the Iron Silk Bureau, believing the Emperor's laws have corrupted the true spirit of martial arts.

## Campaign History

- Nearly signed the "Blood Feud" treaty (a financial/marital alliance) with the Cloud-Tread Syndicate at the Grand Lotus Tea House; the party sabotaged the signing by poisoning the Syndicate's tea, which the Syndicate blamed on the Academy, collapsing the deal into open hostility.
- Two Imperial spies embedded in the Academy are named in the Meridian Scrolls as future rebel removal targets (not yet acted on).
