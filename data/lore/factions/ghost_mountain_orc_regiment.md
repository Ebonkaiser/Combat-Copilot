# Ghost Mountain Monks' Orc Regiment

A small orc military force (staged ~400 miles from the monastery, near the coast between the Principality of Krakenfell and the Holy Seat of Milch) originally assembled by Toru's former handler to march on the Ghost-Mountain Monks' monastery.

**Chain of command:** Orc custom dictates whoever defeats the regiment's leader in the ancient rite of battle assumes command. After Iron/Duke Horn (in his "Iron" transformation) gored the handler and Toru delivered the finishing blow, the regiment transferred its loyalty and command to **Toru and Iron/Duke Horn as joint commanders**. The regiment celebrated with a feast; both were crowd-carried to seats of honor.

**Operational use:** To call them, the commanding officers send a messenger bearing the regiment's seal; the regiment replies with an ETA.

**Secrecy:** The party was warned to keep the regiment's existence — and their command of it — hidden from the Empress, since unauthorized acquisition of an orc military force could have serious political consequences. This is now further complicated by the party's subsequent break from the Empire entirely.
