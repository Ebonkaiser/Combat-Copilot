# Sun-Eater Sect

**Role:** Faction of outcast rebels and practical survivalists born from the starving lower class.

**Location:** South, near Minsh. Settlements include Draxis and the port outpost of Xiaoguanzhou.

**Martial Style — The Scorched Blade:** Unorthodox, brutal street-fighting mixed with blinding powder, smoke pellets, and concealed black-powder explosives. Fast, erratic swordsmanship designed to kill in a single, ungraceful strike.

**Philosophy:** "The high clans speak of honor while sleeping on silk. We survive."

**The Conflict:** Hate the Iron Silk Bureau for their oppression; despise the Jade Orchid Academy for their snobbery. Fund their rebellion through smuggling and raiding merchant caravans, pushing toward open war with everyone.

## The Barge Raid

The Sect hijacked an iron-hulled Imperial supply barge from Gak Lei-Kun, anchoring it at a secluded river bend to unload restricted black powder — the most time-critical of Zhu's three missions. The party infiltrated in disguise, fought through the barracks (2 berserkers) and freed ~10 hostages from the lunchroom, then defeated **Lieutenant Jin the Scorched** and his crew on the bridge, retaking the vessel and returning it to Gak Lei-Kun.

## Prospective Rebel Ally

After U.G., Rose, and Iron/Duke Horn broke from the Empire and joined Adovarius/Ankamideus's cause, the party selected the Sun-Eaters as the **first faction** in its effort to seize the Bureau's regional spy network — the Sect's existing hatred of the Bureau makes it the natural first front.

Two Imperial spies embedded in the Sect were targeted:
1. **Chen Nguyen** ("Master Chen") — operating a hidden apothecary/tunnel network beneath Draxis, guarded by an umber hulk ("Bug George"). Poisoned by Rose; confirmed dead. His Bureau codebook and coded rebel-identity slips were recovered.
2. **Master Ba** — black-powder munitions supplier in Xiaoguanzhou, secretly feeding raid timing/locations to the Bureau. Currently under surveillance; not yet removed.
