# Bureau Codebook

Cipher reference recovered from Chen Nguyen's concealed living quarters beneath his Draxis apothecary (found by U.G. on a stand beside his pallet). Contents are codes/ciphers rather than recipes.

## Use

Deciphers coded messages passed through the Iron Silk Bureau's network, including correspondence to/from Zhu. Paired with coded prescription slips (medical logs + rebel identities) recovered from Chen's body, giving the party material to interpret through the codebook — an active intelligence asset from the party's first spy removal.

## Related Entities
- Chen Nguyen (NPC), Draxis (Location)
