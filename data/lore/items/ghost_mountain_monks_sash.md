# Ghost Mountain Monk's Sash

Textile garment found in a rogue monk's bamboo-forest cave hideout, with a metal mounting point for the amulet recovered from a slain medicine man. Bears the Ghost-Mountain emblem (a ghostly mountain with an X through it). Combined and returned to a wandering pilgrim, who used it to complete the dead monk's regalia before handing it back to the party.

## Current Status

Formally awarded to **Toru** by the Acting Grandmaster as a reward for resolving the orc threat against the monastery. Does not require attunement; grants **advantage on Dexterity saving throws**. Currently equipped by Toru.
