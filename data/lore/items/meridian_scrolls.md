# The Meridian Scrolls

Classified Iron Silk Bureau document listing the real identities of every Imperial spy embedded within the Five Clans. Stolen from a locked safe in a murdered Bureau inspector's study at Gak Lei-Kun (see: that location's entry) — a theft blamed on Adovarius.

## Current Possession & Use

Recovered by the party near Tian Wei (dropped by Shao the Unbroken when knocked from the bamboo). **Never returned to Zhu/the Bureau.** Adovarius repurposed the scrolls as a rebel counterintelligence weapon: they name **12 Imperial spies**, with 6 immediate targets (2 each in the Sun-Eater Sect, Jade Orchid Academy, Cloud-Tread Syndicate) slated for quiet removal and replacement by rebel loyalists, so the rebellion inherits their intelligence access rather than merely burning it.

**Progress:** Chen Nguyen (Sun-Eater) removed — first success. Master Ba (Sun-Eater) under surveillance. Jade Orchid Academy and Cloud-Tread Syndicate targets not yet acted on.
