# The Cipher Wheel

Stolen device capable of decoding Zhu's private letters, taken by an escaped Imperial prisoner also connected to the Meridian Scrolls theft. Tracked north to Tian Wei before the trail went cold.

## Status

Recovered (broken) by Rose near Tian Wei, at the same site Shao the Unbroken dropped the Meridian Scrolls when knocked from the bamboo. **Never returned to Zhu.** With the party's subsequent break from the Empire (Sanctuary of Starlight), returning it is now moot/uncertain.
