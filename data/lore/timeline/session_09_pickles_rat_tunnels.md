# Session 9 — "Pickles and the Rat Tunnels" (2026-08-13)

U.G. was drawn into an astral audience and formally bound to Ankamideus as **Executioner of Her Will**; Iron/Duke Horn knelt and swore a parallel blood oath, replacing his insignia with Ankamideus's balanced tree symbol.

Adovarius turned the recovered Meridian Scrolls into the rebellion's first operation: 12 named Imperial spies, 6 immediate targets (2 each in Sun-Eaters, Jade Orchids, Cloud-Tread). The party chose the Sun-Eaters first and traveled to **Draxis**. After a shopping spree at Chinzo's (Toru: Eldritch Claw Tattoo; Duke: Javelin of Thunder) and passing disguise checks into town, the party's dossier lead took them to Morgan "Pickle" Pickleny's apothecary, who reported "Master Chen" had been asking after them. Rose tailed **Chen Nguyen** to a dilapidated hut, found a concealed trapdoor, and the party discovered his empty hidden underground apothecary.

**Notable quotes:** "I bet this bitch is in everybody's basement listening... We got a rat. Pickles was right." —Rose. "Call me Pickle." —Morgan Pickleny.

**Open threads:** Search Chen's lab and decide how to remove him; continue the spy-removal campaign; prepare for the current Empress noticing the broken pacts.
