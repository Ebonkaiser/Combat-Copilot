# Session 8 — "Hay, Blades, and Heresy" (2026-08-06) — MAJOR TURNING POINT

Following Adovarius's directions into the Sacred Forest, the party chased Shao the Unbroken through an ancient magical veil into the hidden **Sanctuary of Starlight**. Iron's transformation triggered mid-chase (calmed again with hay by Toru). The party survived the sanctuary's lethal trials — swinging blades, a 50-foot animated statue, trapped bridges, a false-tripwire cistern — reaching Adovarius, Shao, and robed acolytes at the temple's heart.

Adovarius claimed **Ankamideus (the True Empress of Light)** is an ancient crystal dragon banished by Milin, and that the current Empress's line suppresses magic to preserve stolen power. **U.G. broke his pact with the current Empress and accepted Ankamideus**, persuading Iron/Duke Horn and Rose to join. Adovarius healed the party, became their new handler, and the group reached level 4.

**Notable quotes:** "Guys, this shit's fucked." —Rose. "We may have gotten things wrong from the get-go." —U.G.

**Open threads:** Learn more about Ankamideus and a path to reach her; manage consequences of the broken Imperial pacts; decide how far to take the rebellion.
