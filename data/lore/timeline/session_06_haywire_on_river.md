# Session 6 — "Haywire on the River" (2026-07-16)

Finished the barge fight: Iron (transformed) gored a berserker with a natural 20; Toru killed the crippled berserker, an archer, and finally Jin himself with a punch. Barge secured. Iron then panicked off the ship with Toru clinging to him — **Toru discovered hay calms the transformation**, and the two flew back via Duke's winged boots. Rose and U.G. piloted the barge back to Gak Lei-Kun.

Prioritized Master Kang Chen's worsening illness over the cipher-wheel escapee. At the Grand Lotus Tea House, Kang Chen described two poisonings, the second after a secret Jade Orchid meeting. Investigation (Duke's nose, Toru's search of a loose bandolier) uncovered a hidden basement laboratory and captured its resident, **Hanzo the Bitter Draught** — who answered only in riddles and extortion threats.

**Notable quotes:** "Horses use horsepower." —Rose. "There's somebody living in the walls!" —Rose.

**Open threads:** Interrogate Hanzo; clear the party's name with the Syndicate; identify the poison/antidote; manage Iron's transformation risk going forward (hay confirmed as the fix).
