# Session 2 — "A Single Tear Rolls Down His Face" (2026-06-10)

Investigated the blood-draining monk's cave hideout: recovered a coded journal and a sash with an amulet setting. A wandering pilgrim identified the monk as a Ghost-Mountain outcast (expelled possibly for his gorilla companion, forbidden techniques, or the Grandmaster's assassination). Rose's natural-20 charisma check calmed the grieving albino ape; the pilgrim completed the sash/amulet and mourned his old companion.

**Shaken down on the road:** Four Cloud-Tread Syndicate bounty hunters intercepted the party, demanding 500 silver each for a silent bounty tied to the tea house sabotage. U.G. fought and lost; the party paid.

**The locked-room murder:** Zhu assigned two new missions. At Gak Lei-Kun, the party investigated Bureau inspector Vahn's murder (chest caved by a single palm strike, Meridian Scrolls stolen from his safe). Evidence pointed to Ghost-Mountain iron-body conditioning and Cloud-Tread lockpicking wire. A sulfur-scented Draconic note implicated the escaped mage **Adovarius**, who had broken out of Verdant Meridian custody.

**Notable quotes:** "You may have beaten me once, but I am now stronger... May the true Empress of light rise again." —Adovarius, via note.

**Open threads:** Track Adovarius (~400 miles northeast); recover the Meridian Scrolls; investigate dying Tian Wei; the Cloud-Tread bounty remains unresolved.
