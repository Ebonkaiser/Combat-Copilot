# Session 5 — "Board the Barge, Brothers" (2026-07-08)

Chose the time-critical barge mission. Stocked up at Chinzo's (finally learned to haggle; U.G. traded gems for 18 health potions and commissioned a Helm of Comprehend Languages for 5,000 silver). After a river journey up the Honglong and a bribed fisherman's directions, the party found the hijacked iron-hulled barge.

Iron/Duke Horn disguised the group as Sun-Eater Sect members. Bluffed aboard; cleared the barracks (killed/subdued 2 berserkers after a failed bluff — U.G. landed a natural-20 Booming Blade for 32 damage), freed ~10 hostages from the lunchroom, and looted 4,000 silver/gems. Reaching the bridge, they came face to face with **Lieutenant Jin the Scorched**, who saw through their disguise and demanded they unmask. Session ended on the brink of battle.

**Notable quotes:** "This place smells like shit. Let's loot it." —Rose. "You take me for a fool? Take off your masks." —Lieutenant Jin.

**Open threads:** Defeat Jin's crew; handle the black powder; extract the hostages; Iron/Duke Horn began the next session already transformed.
