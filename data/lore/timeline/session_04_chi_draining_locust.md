# Session 4 — "The Chi-Draining Locust of Tian Wei" (2026-07-01)

Ichidori returned from his audience with the Empress, teleported back in a blaze of holy fire. Entering Tian Wei, the party's ki grew cold and sluggish; Duke's Divine Sense traced the disturbance underground to a long-dry well. Descending into an Underdark passage: fought venomous "danger noodles" (9 Con saves for Toru), found a betrayal-vision mosaic and a treasure cache, then destroyed a **ki-draining locust** on a stone pedestal — instantly lifting the village's affliction. Tian Wei healed; the sun returned.

On the road back, the party interrupted Cloud-Tread Syndicate mercenaries robbing Chinzo, confirmed the Syndicate as the bounty's source, and killed all three.

Reported to Zhu, who issued three new missions: recover a stolen cipher wheel (also possibly holding the Meridian Scroll), retake a hijacked Imperial barge from the Sun-Eater Sect (most urgent), and protect the dying Master Kang Chen.

**Notable quotes:** "I have come before you, Empress, humble and contrite, to accept your word." —U.G. "Hard way, motherfuckers, let's go!" —Rose.

**Open threads:** Barge rescue (time-critical); pursue the cipher-wheel escapee; protect Kang Chen; resolve the Syndicate bounty.
