# Session 1 — "Tea, Poison, and Opportunity Attacks" (2026-06-04)

Fresh off subduing Adovarius, the party received two new assignments from a representative of the Empress of Light: sabotage the peace treaty between the Jade Orchid Academy and the Cloud-Tread Syndicate, and hunt a creature terrorizing supply lines through the bamboo forest.

**Treaty sabotage:** Infiltrated the Grand Lotus Tea House in Zhong Gong disguised as kitchen/serving staff. Ichidori slipped toxic flowers into the communal tea water; Rose served it to the Cloud-Tread delegation. The resulting illness caused the Syndicate to accuse the Jade Orchid Academy of poisoning, collapsing the treaty into open conflict. Party escaped clean.

**The Jiangshi:** Camping on the Song Gong Trail, the party was ambushed by a blood-draining martial artist and a large albino ape companion attacking a medicine man's wagon. The figure was defeated (medicine man did not survive); the ape fled. Investigation revealed the attacker wore Ghost Mountain Monk robes. A mountain-peak amulet was recovered from the medicine man's body along with 4 healing potions and a keystone.

**Notable quotes:** "They're not merely for decoration." —Zu, handing over mission scrolls. "We need items to fight a ghost. What do you have?" —Rose, to Chinzo.

**Open threads:** Nature of the blood-drainer and its connection to the Ghost Mountain Monks; the fleeing albino ape; reporting the Blood Feud sabotage for payment; party nearing level 3.
