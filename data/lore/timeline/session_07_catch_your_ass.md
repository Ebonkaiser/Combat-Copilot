# Session 7 — "Catch Your Ass" (2026-07-22)

With Kang Chen minutes from death, the party broke Hanzo's sealed antidote scrolls with Rose's poison; U.G. read the paired texts via his Helm of Comprehend Languages. Brewed and administered the cure just in time — Kang Chen survived, refusing Hanzo's 50,000-silver extortion. A Cloud-Tread "vice lieutenant" then took Hanzo and the tea leaves into custody without a confession. In the abandoned lab, Rose upgraded her poisoner's kit and U.G. found a hidden map of the tea house's secret routes.

North of Zhong Gong, a burned courier cart yielded a sulfur-scented Draconic note pointing to a rendezvous near Tian Wei. There, **Shao the Unbroken** — an escaped Verdant Meridian prisoner and Adovarius's acolyte — ambushed and then fled the party through smoke and bamboo, eventually knocked from the trees by Rose's crossbow. He escaped custody twice, but left behind **Zhu's broken cipher and the stolen Meridian Scrolls**, which the party recovered.

**Notable quotes:** "We got antidotes at home." —Rose. "Fuck you! Caught your ass!" —Rose.

**Open threads:** Follow Adovarius's new coordinates; decide what to do with the recovered Meridian Scrolls and cipher; assess the Cloud-Tread lieutenant's trustworthiness.
