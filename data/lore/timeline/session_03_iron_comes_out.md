# Session 3 — "Iron Comes Out to Play" (2026-06-23)

Ichidori vanished, summoned by the Empress. A messenger's scroll ("an old friend awaits," seal matching Toru's amulet) led the party to the Ghost-Mountain Monks' monastery. The Acting Grandmaster revealed Toru was once an orc assassin reformed by the late Grandmaster — and that Toru's old handler was marching a regiment of orcs to reclaim him, threatening the party's execution if the monks fell.

Stopped at Chinzo's for Iron/Duke Horn's commissioned horn caps (Lightning element, 10,000 silver). At the orc camp, Toru surrendered himself; the handler revealed his true intent to kill everyone regardless, triggering the orc rite of ritual combat. **Iron (Duke Horn's secondary personality) emerged for the first time**, goring the handler — then turning on Toru, who subdued him. Toru revived the handler with Hand of Healing and finished him with a palm strike. The orcs acclaimed Toru and Iron/Duke Horn as new joint commanders.

On the road home, the party destroyed an undead chi-draining creature. Returned to the monastery: rewards given (5,000 silver each, Toru's sash, Rose's +1 short sword, wagon repaired).

**Notable quotes:** "No orc should die a coward's death like that. We can finish this now." —Toru. "Out of character? I was coming here to die, bitch." —Toru.

**Open threads:** Investigate dying Tian Wei; track Adovarius; manage the new orc regiment (keep hidden from the Empress); help Duke control Iron via meditation.
