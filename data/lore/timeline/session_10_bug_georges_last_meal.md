# Session 10 — "Bug George's Last Meal" (2026-08-19/20) — MOST RECENT SESSION

Pushed deeper into Chen Nguyen's tunnels and fought his guardian, an umber hulk immediately named **Bug George** by Rose. After a grueling close-quarters fight (Toru nearly overwhelmed by poison gas and claws), Bug George fell. The exposed apothecary yielded antidotes, a healer's kit, poison ingredients (including a slow-acting lethal toxin), silver, and a Necklace of Prayer Beads (attuned by Duke). U.G. found Chen's private quarters and his **Bureau codebook** — a major intelligence asset.

Rather than an open confrontation, Rose poisoned Chen's food and water; the party returned after resting to find him dead. Recovered his insignia and coded prescription slips (rebel identities), reported the confirmed kill via Sending Stone, hid his body in Bug George's remains, and collapsed the tunnel with black powder — **the rebellion's first completed spy removal**.

Traveled to **Xiaoguanzhou** and identified the next target, **Master Ba**, a bombastic black-powder supplier double-dealing with the Bureau. Tailed him to a hidden bamboo cache and made camp nearby to observe rather than strike immediately.

**Notable quotes:** "Why the fuck would he keep that as a pet?... That's a bug man." —Rose. "Boys, he fucking ate it." —Rose. "The eagle has landed. Confirm the eagle has landed." —U.G.

**Open threads (current, as of last session):** Remove Master Ba without losing his supply chain; use the Bureau codebook and coded slips to decode Imperial traffic; continue replacing the remaining 5 named Imperial spies; deploy the orc regiment if needed; find a route to Ankamideus's prison realm.
