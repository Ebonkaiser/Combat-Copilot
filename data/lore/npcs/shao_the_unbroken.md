# Shao the Unbroken

Qinggong practitioner, one of the few to escape the Verdant Meridian holding facility. Carries a faint sulfur scent linking him to Adovarius's Draconic correspondence. Extraordinarily mobile — smoke pellets, darts, bamboo-canopy evasion.

## Encounters

**First (bamboo rendezvous near Tian Wei):** Knew the party by name; fled rather than explain himself. Knocked unconscious and bound, but escaped twice — once via a concealed smoke device, once amid a released hornet swarm plus more smoke. Before fleeing he claimed to possess "the cipher" and to be en route to meet his handler. Rose recovered Zhu's broken cipher and the stolen Meridian Scrolls where he fell.

**Second (Sacred Forest):** Spotted at a campfire; fled through the bamboo when the party approached. Wounded by Rose's crossbow. The chase drove Iron/Duke Horn's transformation and led the group through an ancient magical veil into the hidden **Sanctuary of Starlight**, where Shao was later found alongside Adovarius and other robed acolytes — confirming his place in Adovarius's movement.
