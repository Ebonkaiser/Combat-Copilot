# Chen Nguyen ("Master Chen")

**Status: Deceased — first confirmed rebel spy removal.**

Imperial spy embedded among the Sun-Eaters, named in the Meridian Scrolls. Posed as an unassuming older street alchemist in Draxis — hunched, gray-robed, scraggly beard, healthy hair, alchemical supply pouch.

## Discovery

Morgan Pickleny (apothecary owner) reported "Master Chen" had been asking after a minotaur, a young girl, and two humanoids — he was already hunting the disguised party. Rose spotted and tailed him to a dilapidated hut; he vanished into an empty upper room. Rose found a concealed trapdoor; Iron/Duke Horn tracked alchemical scent through the tunnels to Chen's hidden underground apothecary, guarded by an umber hulk the party named **Bug George**.

## Removal

After killing Bug George and finding Chen's private quarters, the party poisoned his cheese, bread, peanut butter, and water with a slow-acting lethal toxin recovered from his own lab, then let him return to it. Found dead beside his half-eaten meal the next morning. The party recovered his apothecary hat/insignia, coded prescription slips (medical logs + rebel identities), and his **Bureau codebook**. Reported the confirmed kill via Sending Stone; body was hidden inside Bug George's corpse and the tunnel collapsed with black powder. A rebel replacement is being dispatched to inherit his position.
