# The Acting Grandmaster

Current leader of the Ghost-Mountain Monks, serving in place of the late Grandmaster (murdered under unclear circumstances). Appears humble but carries unmistakable authority; young for the position but noted as wise.

## Key Interactions

- Recognized the scroll that summoned the party as written by his own late master (the same wandering monk who reformed Toru).
- Held **Toru personally responsible** for the orc threat and made clear the party would be executed by the monks themselves if the monastery fell.
- Arranged for two acolytes to give the party additional mission context.
- On the party's success (orc handler defeated, no bloodshed at the gates): declared **Toru an ally of the Ghost-Mountain Monks**, awarded 5,000 silver each, gifted Toru the Ghost Mountain Monks' Sash, gave Rose a +1 short sword, and repaired the party's wagon.
