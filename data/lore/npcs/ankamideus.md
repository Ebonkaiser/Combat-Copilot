# Ankamideus ("Anka") — The True Empress of Light

A shapeshifting Ancient Crystal Dragon, ~1,500+ years old, the original rightful owner of the divine flame. True form: Huge, Chaotic Neutral draconic being of psionic and radiant power; most commonly shapeshifts into a beautiful woman.

## The Founding Legend

Legend says the first emperor, Milin, found her injured and nursed her to health. She fell in love with him and gifted him the divine flame (a sample of her innate magic power) and a staff containing her magic; their marriage founded the empire. After years of peace, Anka disappeared — driving Milin mad and causing him to restrict all magic in the kingdom.

**Adovarius's revised account:** Anka was not lost — Milin himself learned to harness the Eternal Flame, bound her, and banished her to another realm. The current Empress descends from Milin and suppresses magic to preserve authority stolen from Anka.

## Pacts

- **U.G. (Ichidori)** broke his pact with the current Empress and accepted Ankamideus as patron, later formalized as **"Executioner of Her Will"** — bound to hunt murderers and oathbreakers, restore outlawed magic, dethrone tyrants, and show no leniency to proven threats (peace through retribution).
- **Iron/Duke Horn** swore a blood oath, rejecting forgiveness as weakness, and took Ankamideus's balanced living-and-dead tree as his new sacred symbol (replacing his former Paladin insignia).
- Reaching/rescuing Ankamideus from her extradimensional prison may require a spelljammer or planar magic (e.g., Dimension Door).
