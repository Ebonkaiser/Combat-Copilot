# Hanzo the Bitter Draught

Evasive occupant of a hidden laboratory beneath the Grand Lotus Tea House, discovered after Toru exposed a concealed stairway behind a loose wall-mounted bandolier. His workspace: herbs, compounds, vials, recipe scrolls, and a lived-in sleeping area, lit by luminescent mushrooms.

## Capture and Interrogation

Captured by Toru (through a poisonous cloud) and dragged upstairs. Answers only in riddles ("a forgotten foundling, a ragged wretch wrapped in rain and hunger..."), refusing to name his employer. Demanded 50,000 silver and safe passage in exchange for Master Kang Chen's life, threatening Chen would die within 5 minutes.

The party instead broke his sealed antidote scrolls (marked with a living-and-dead tree sigil) using Rose's poison to trigger the seal, brewed the cure from his own supplies, and saved Kang Chen before the deadline — refusing the extortion.

## Resolution

A purported Cloud-Tread Syndicate vice lieutenant arrived with three attendants and took Hanzo and the poisoned tea leaves into custody. **He never confessed or named his employer.** Whether this custody transfer clears the party of suspicion, and who actually hired him, remains unresolved.
