# The Orc Handler

**Status: Deceased.**

Commanding officer who originally dispatched Toru on his assassination mission against the Ghost-Mountain Monks' Grandmaster, expecting Toru to complete the mission or die trying. When Toru instead survived and settled among the monks, the handler assembled a regiment of orcs and marched to reclaim him — vowing to raid the monastery regardless of outcome.

## Death

At the orc camp, Toru surrendered himself to protect the monks, but the handler revealed he intended to kill the party and raid the monastery anyway. Toru attacked, triggering the ancient orc rite of ritual combat. Iron/Duke Horn's "Iron" personality emerged mid-fight and gored the handler, who began bleeding out. Toru — unwilling to let an orc die a coward's death — revived him with Hand of Healing, then killed him a second and final time with a palm strike to the solar plexus (the same technique the Grandmaster once used on Toru).

His death transferred command of the regiment to Toru and Iron/Duke Horn.
