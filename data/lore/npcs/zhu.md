# Zhu

Tall, lanky, formal. Direct liaison to the (current) Empress of Light — not a Bureau member himself, but the party's primary contact for assignments. Operates from concealed forest meeting points via Verdant Meridian Wardens; keeps meticulous scroll records.

## History with the Party

- Briefed the party on the Five Clans (Iron Silk Bureau, Sun-Eater Sect, Jade Orchid Academy, Cloud-Tread Syndicate, Ghost-Mountain Monks) and assigned recovery of the Meridian Scrolls + investigation of Tian Wei.
- Refused to intervene in the Cloud-Tread Syndicate's silent bounty on the party ("not his department").
- After Tian Wei, issued three new mission scrolls: recover the stolen cipher wheel, retake the hijacked barge (most urgent), and protect Master Kang Chen.
- Rejected U.G.'s jesting marriage proposal with a warning to "refrain or face the consequences."

## Current Status

**Relationship severed.** After the Sanctuary of Starlight, U.G. broke his pact with the current Empress and bound himself to Ankamideus; Rose and Iron/Duke Horn joined the anti-Imperial cause. The party still holds Zhu's recovered broken cipher and the Meridian Scrolls. Zhu and the Bureau are now former authorities, not allies — Adovarius has replaced Zhu as the party's handler.
