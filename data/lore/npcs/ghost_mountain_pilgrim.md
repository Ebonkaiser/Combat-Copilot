# The Ghost Mountain Pilgrim

Unnamed, gaunt wanderer found cleaning and eulogizing the body of the slain rogue monk near his bamboo-forest cave. Initially reluctant to talk, he opened up once the party produced the monk's amulet.

## Information Provided

Confirmed the dead monk was a nameless Ghost-Mountain outcast (order custom: no names) possibly expelled for one or more of: his attachment to a baby albino gorilla companion, pursuit of forbidden techniques (including the blood-draining art used on the Song Gong Trail), or suspected involvement in the Grandmaster's assassination. Described him as having "a real thirst for knowledge — especially the forbidden stuff."

## Resolution

Took the recovered amulet, fitted it into the monk's sash (assembling it into a traveling outfit from his own pack), returned the completed item to the party, and sat in silent meditation over his old companion's body alongside the grieving albino ape.
