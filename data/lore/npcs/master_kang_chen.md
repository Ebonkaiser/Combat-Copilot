# Master Kang Chen

Cloud-Tread Syndicate leader and merchant prince. Received the party at the Grand Lotus Tea House with suspicion — someone matching their descriptions was seen near the tea house when he fell ill.

## The Poisonings

Two separate bouts: the first caused vomiting/discomfort (recovered over ~a week); the second, far more severe (weak, vomiting, bleeding), began after a **clandestine nighttime meeting with Jade Orchid Academy delegates** — a meeting only directly involved parties should have known about. He directed the party to investigate the kitchen and staff.

The investigation led to Hanzo the Bitter Draught's hidden laboratory. When Hanzo threatened Kang Chen's life for ransom, the party brewed an antidote from Hanzo's own sealed scrolls and Rose administered it in time — Kang Chen's color returned and he recovered enough to sit upright, though still weak.

## Status

A Cloud-Tread vice lieutenant subsequently took custody of Hanzo and the tainted tea leaves. Whether Kang Chen's recovery/testimony actually clears the party of the Syndicate's silent bounty remains unresolved.
