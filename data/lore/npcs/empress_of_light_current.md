# Empress of Light (current ruler of Milin)

Little is known about her by custom. Sole owner and controller of the divine flame — an artifact acting as a conduit to the gods, bestowing her mystical powers.

Adovarius claims she is a direct descendant of Milin and that her regime's magic prohibition exists to preserve authority originally stolen from Ankamideus, the First Empress.

## Status

During the Sanctuary of Starlight confrontation, U.G.'s pact seal to her broke when he renounced her service for Ankamideus. Rose and Iron/Duke Horn also chose to support dethroning her. She is now aware the party's pact seals have broken and cannot presently scry or locate them — but their return to Imperial notice poses an immediate risk as the covert anti-Imperial campaign begins.
