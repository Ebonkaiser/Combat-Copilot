# Master Ba

**Status: Active target — under surveillance, not yet removed.**

Loud, bombastic supplier of black-powder munitions operating from the bay town of Xiaoguanzhou. Second of two Imperial spies named in the Meridian Scrolls embedded in the Sun-Eater Sect. Sells explosives to the Sun-Eaters while secretly passing the Iron Silk Bureau the expected times and locations of rebel raids (inferred from the deliveries he arranges).

## Current Situation

Found loudly advertising his wares in a Xiaoguanzhou tavern. The disguised party (Rose and Iron/Duke Horn as homeless travelers, Toru as an old man, U.G. as a rogue trader) waited for him to leave and tailed him in staggered pairs into the bamboo. Rose traced his scattered black-powder trail to a concealed enclosure where he was filling bottles from barrels, singing to himself. The party withdrew ~200 feet and made camp to observe his operation before deciding how to remove him **without losing access to his supply chain**.
