# Adovarius

Tall, dark-haired mage in long blue robes, always carrying a grimoire. Initially neutral-aligned; turned dark after a mob discovered his magic and murdered his family.

## Role in the Campaign

- **Prime suspect** in the Meridian Scrolls theft and the murder of Inspector Vahn at Gak Lei-Kun (locked-room, single palm-strike kill). A Draconic note found on Vahn's body — sulfur-scented, matching Adovarius's hand — read: *"You may have beaten me once, but I am now stronger. You will pay for what you've done. We will meet again. May the true Empress of light rise again."*
- Escaped from the Verdant Meridian holding facility before the party could re-question him; last tracked ~400 miles northeast near Tian Wei.
- Sent further sulfur-scented Draconic messages, one referencing Zhu's Cipher Wheel and directing the party to a rendezvous where they met his acolyte **Shao the Unbroken**.
- Ultimately revealed himself at the hidden **Sanctuary of Starlight**, alongside Shao and other robed acolytes. He claims **Ankamideus** (the First Empress of Light, an ancient crystal dragon) was deliberately bound and banished to another realm by Milin after he took her Eternal Flame — and that the current Empress, Milin's descendant, suppresses magic to preserve stolen authority.
- U.G. accepted this account, broke his pact with the current Empress, and took a new pact with Ankamideus; Rose and Iron/Duke Horn joined the cause. Adovarius healed the party and became their **new handler**.

## As Handler

Turned the recovered Meridian Scrolls into a rebel weapon: of 12 named Imperial spies, 6 are immediate targets (2 each in Sun-Eater Sect, Jade Orchid Academy, Cloud-Tread Syndicate) to be quietly removed and replaced with loyalists, seizing their intelligence channels. Provided a Sending Stone and confirmed the current Empress cannot presently scry/locate the party. Confirmed the first success (Chen Nguyen's removal in Draxis) and the party's move to the second Sun-Eater target, Master Ba.
