# U.G. (Ichidori / Yuji Ichidori)

**Class/Race:** Rogue (Warlock pact), Elf — Male — Player Character

## Core Concept

Opportunistic, theatrical rogue whose instinct for tradecraft and deception gradually became a search for a cause worth risking himself for. Estranged from his mother's mercantile trading-family legacy after rejecting an apprenticeship.

## Pact History

- Originally swore fealty to the **current Empress of Light** as her sworn agent (Warlock pact, level 2): hunt the corrupted, guard her light, uphold the magic ban. Was once literally summoned into her presence mid-campaign for "brazen use of magic."
- At the Sanctuary of Starlight, became **the first of the party to defect** — broke this pact and swore a new one to **Ankamideus**, later formalized as **"Executioner of Her Will"**: punish tyrants, oathbreakers, and murderers; restore outlawed magic; show no leniency to proven threats.

## Equipment

Rapier (haggled from Chinzo); Staff of the Adder (animates into a venomous weapon); Helm of Comprehend Languages (attuned); Book of Shadows (via Ankamideus pact); Zhu's broken cipher; the Meridian Scrolls; Chen Nguyen's Bureau codebook.

## Arc Summary

- Pulled the original sulfur-scented Draconic note from the murdered Bureau inspector's pocket, first linking Adovarius to the "true Empress of Light" conspiracy.
- Landed the campaign's biggest single hit: a natural-20 Booming Blade sneak attack for 32 damage against a Sun-Eater berserker on the hijacked barge.
- His Helm of Comprehend Languages was pivotal in reading Hanzo's antidote-recipe scrolls in time to save Master Kang Chen.
- Now applying his tradecraft to the rebellion's spy-removal operations: found Chen Nguyen's hidden Bureau codebook, the intelligence coup underpinning the first successful removal.
