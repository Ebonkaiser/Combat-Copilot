# Rose (Rose Shi)

**Class/Race:** Fighter, Human — Female, 15 years old — Player Character

## Core Concept

Wary, resourceful young fighter, homeless and self-reliant for years before the party. Fights with a crossbow and poisoner's instincts; her real strength is lateral thinking — spotting false walls, hidden mechanisms, and political weaknesses others miss. Dry, deadpan humor; willing to use ugly methods (poison, disguise, misdirection) without being cruel — she just grew up without clean options.

**Portrait:** Lanky, tanned, dark hair, ragged robes and a light cape.

## Equipment

Hand crossbow; ornate hand crossbow (attuned — casts Scorching Ray at level 2, once/day); +1 short sword (Ghost-Mountain gift); upgraded poisoner's kit (advantage on related checks); Bracers of Archery.

## Arc Summary

- Formerly an agent of the current Empress. At the Sanctuary of Starlight, revealed she was cast out of the palace as a child and separated from her mother — this personal wound reframed as part of Ankamideus's alleged imperial betrayal, prompting her ("damn the man") to join U.G. and Iron/Duke Horn against the current Empress.
- Signature tactics: poisoned the Cloud-Tread Syndicate's tea to sabotage the Blood Feud treaty; poisoned Chen Nguyen's food/water for the party's first quiet spy removal; broke Hanzo's sealed antidote scrolls with her own poison to save Master Kang Chen.
- Recovered the Meridian Scrolls and Zhu's broken cipher wheel from the site of Shao the Unbroken's escape.
- Now applying survival/poisoner skills to the deliberate insurgency against embedded Imperial spies (Sun-Eater front).
