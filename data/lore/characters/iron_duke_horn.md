# Iron / Duke Horn

**Class/Race:** Paladin, Minotaur — Male — Player Character

## Core Concept

A formidable Minotaur Paladin defined by the conflict between his protective conscience and **Iron**, a violent secondary identity that emerges under extreme pressure (typically triggered by sustained combat stress/drumming/chaos). In battle he's a durable frontliner; outside it, unexpectedly attentive — his Divine Sense and keen nose repeatedly surface threats others miss (sulfur on a murdered inspector's note, fading warmth near Tian Wei's ki-drain, poison beneath the Grand Lotus Tea House).

## Equipment

Great Club; ornate horn caps (attuned, Fire — element changeable each dawn, currently element varies per session; 1d6 fire on gore / 1d4 channeled into weapon attacks); Winged Boots (flight = walking speed, up to 4 hrs); Javelin of Thunder (returning, bonus lightning damage); Necklace of Prayer Beads (attuned); Lay on Hands; Divine Sense; Bracers of Archery (+2 ranged damage, longbow/shortbow proficiency).

## Arc Summary

- Discovered his split personality mid-campaign; Iron first fully emerged at an orc encampment, goring the handler responsible for hunting Toru, then turning on Toru himself before being stopped.
- Confessed his condition to Toru, who offered meditation techniques as a possible path to control.
- Iron has resurfaced repeatedly under stress (barge raid, Sanctuary of Starlight); **hay** (fed by Toru) has become the party's reliable method for calming him back to Duke.
- Formally broke with the current Empress at the Sanctuary of Starlight, kneeling before Ankamideus and swearing a blood oath — his old insignia replaced with her balanced living-and-dead tree symbol. Now fights as part of the anti-Imperial rebellion.
- Joint commander (with Toru) of the Ghost-Mountain Monks' orc regiment.
- Notable investigative/rescue moments: recovered antidote scrolls from Hanzo's lab under trap conditions; carried critical evidence via winged-boot flight multiple times; disguised the whole party as Sun-Eater members using makeup/costuming skill.
