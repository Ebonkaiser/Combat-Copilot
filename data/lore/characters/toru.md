# Toru

**Class/Race:** Monk, Orc — Male — Player Character

## Core Concept

Imposing orc monk shaped by two lives: a criminal past and the discipline his wandering Ghost-Mountain master taught him. Man of few words, decisive action, a personal code valuing a clean ending and earned respect. Became Iron/Duke Horn's most reliable anchor during his transformations (discovered hay calms him).

**Background:** Grew up into crime; beaten and reformed by an old wandering master who turned out to be the Ghost-Mountain Monks' own Grandmaster (whom Toru had originally been sent to assassinate).

**Portrait:** Sumo-wrestler build, 8 feet tall, green skin, chonmage hairstyle, classic martial arts uniform.

## Equipment

Ghost Mountain Monk robes (recovered from the slain rogue monk); Ghost Mountain Monks' Sash (advantage on Dex saves); Eldritch Claw Tattoo (unarmed strikes count as magical, strengthened).

## Arc Summary

- His past caught up when his old handler marched an orc regiment on the Ghost-Mountain monastery to reclaim him. Toru surrendered himself to protect the monks ("I came here to die"), but the handler intended to kill everyone regardless.
- In the resulting ritual combat, Iron/Duke Horn's transformation gored the handler to death; Toru revived him with Hand of Healing purely to kill him properly with the Grandmaster's own palm-strike technique — earning the orc regiment's respect and joint command (with Iron/Duke Horn).
- Did not take the same dramatic oath as U.G. and Iron/Duke Horn at the Sanctuary of Starlight, but chose to support the rebellion's practical work regardless — removing Imperial spies quietly (Chen Nguyen, Draxis) rather than through grand declarations.
- Consistently the party's "quiet exit" specialist — found the hidden pressure-plate exit from Hanzo's sealed lab in time to save Master Kang Chen; recognized Cloud-Tread hierarchy and Ghost-Mountain iron-body evidence at crime scenes.
