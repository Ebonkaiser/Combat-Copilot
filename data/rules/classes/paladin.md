# Paladin (Class Basics)

Hit Die: d10. Saving throw proficiencies: Wisdom, Charisma. Primary abilities: Strength, Charisma.

## Core Features (by level, abbreviated)

- **Divine Sense (1st level):** As an action, detect the location of any celestial, fiend, or undead within 60 feet not behind total cover, and know its type (not identity). Can also detect the presence/location of a place or object consecrated/desecrated by a Hallow spell. Usable a limited number of times, regaining uses on a long rest.
- **Lay on Hands (1st level):** A pool of healing power (5 × Paladin level) usable as an action to restore HP to a creature touched, drawn from the pool. Can also spend 5 points to cure one disease or neutralize one poison instead of healing.
- **Fighting Style (2nd level):** Choose a combat specialization (e.g., Defense, Dueling, Great Weapon Fighting, Protection).
- **Spellcasting (2nd level):** Paladins are half-casters; prepare spells from the Paladin list using Charisma as spellcasting ability.
- **Divine Smite (2nd level):** When you hit with a melee weapon attack, expend a spell slot to deal extra radiant damage (2d8 for a 1st-level slot, +1d8 per slot level above 1st, max 5d8; +1d8 more if the target is undead or a fiend).
- **Divine Health (3rd level):** Immune to disease.
- **Sacred Oath (3rd level):** Choose a subclass oath (e.g., Devotion, Ancients, Vengeance) that grants additional spells and features, and Channel Divinity options.
- **Extra Attack (5th level):** Attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action.
- **Aura of Protection (6th level):** You and friendly creatures within 10 feet add your Charisma modifier to saving throws.

## Campaign Note

Iron/Duke Horn's oath and insignia changed mid-campaign: he broke from his original order and swore a blood oath to Ankamideus, replacing his prior sacred symbol with her balanced living-and-dead tree. Mechanically this is best represented as a reflavored Sacred Oath (narratively "Oath of Retribution" in spirit) rather than a rules change — his class features remain the same, only the fiction and future Channel Divinity flavor shift to serve Ankamideus's creed of "peace through retribution."
