# Rogue (Class Basics)

Hit Die: d8. Saving throw proficiencies: Dexterity, Intelligence. Primary ability: Dexterity.

## Core Features (by level, abbreviated)

- **Expertise (1st level):** Choose two skill proficiencies (or one skill + thieves' tools); double proficiency bonus for checks using them.
- **Sneak Attack (1st level):** Once per turn, deal extra damage (scaling with Rogue level, e.g., 1d6 at 1st, up 1d6 every two levels) to one creature hit with a finesse or ranged weapon, provided you have advantage on the attack roll, OR another enemy of the target is within 5 feet of it and you don't have disadvantage.
- **Thieves' Cant (1st level):** A secret mix of dialect, jargon, and code.
- **Cunning Action (2nd level):** Bonus action each turn to Dash, Disengage, or Hide.
- **Roguish Archetype (3rd level):** Choose a subclass (e.g., Thief, Assassin, Arcane Trickster).
- **Uncanny Dodge (5th level):** Use your reaction to halve the damage of an attack that hits you.
- **Evasion (7th level):** On a Dexterity save that would normally halve damage (e.g., a Fireball), take no damage on a success and half on a failure.

## Multiclass / Warlock Pact Note

Ichidori (U.G.) is played with rogue skills layered under a **Warlock-style pact** — first with the current Empress of Light, later broken and replaced with a pact to Ankamideus (title: "Executioner of Her Will"). Mechanically, this is best modeled as either a Warlock multiclass dip or a reflavored Rogue subclass granting pact-magic-like features (Eldritch Blast/Booming Blade-style cantrips, a Pact Boon item such as the Book of Shadows, and expanded spell known lists tied to the patron). Note that spells like Eldritch Blast are core Warlock-class SRD content; cantrips such as Booming Blade are **not** part of the SRD (they originate in later WotC supplements) and are represented here only as already-established character abilities from play, not reproduced rules text.
