# Fighter (Class Basics)

Hit Die: d10. Saving throw proficiencies: Strength, Constitution. Primary ability: Strength or Dexterity.

## Core Features (by level, abbreviated)

- **Fighting Style (1st level):** Choose a combat specialization (e.g., Archery: +2 to attack rolls with ranged weapons; Defense: +1 AC while wearing armor; Dueling: +2 damage with a one-handed melee weapon wielded with nothing in the other hand).
- **Second Wind (1st level):** Bonus action, once per short/long rest, to regain 1d10 + Fighter level hit points.
- **Action Surge (2nd level):** Once per short/long rest, take one additional action on your turn.
- **Martial Archetype (3rd level):** Choose a subclass (e.g., Champion, Battle Master, Eldritch Knight) that grants additional combat features.
- **Extra Attack (5th level):** Attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn (further Fighter levels increase this further at higher levels).
- **Indomitable (9th level):** Reroll a failed saving throw, once per long rest.

## Campaign Note

Rose (Rose Shi) fights primarily with a hand crossbow, favoring ranged engagement, poison, and battlefield misdirection over frontline melee — consistent with an Archery-leaning Fighting Style and a build that leans on positioning and preparation rather than raw Extra Attack damage output.
