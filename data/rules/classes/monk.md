# Monk (Class Basics)

Hit Die: d8. Saving throw proficiencies: Strength, Dexterity. Primary abilities: Dexterity, Wisdom.

## Core Features (by level, abbreviated)

- **Unarmored Defense (1st level):** While wearing no armor and no shield, AC = 10 + Dexterity modifier + Wisdom modifier.
- **Martial Arts (1st level):** Unarmed strikes and monk weapons use a d4 (scaling up with level) in place of normal damage if that's higher, and can use Dexterity instead of Strength for the attack/damage rolls. When you take the Attack action with an unarmed strike or monk weapon, you can make one unarmed strike as a bonus action.
- **Ki (2nd level):** A pool of points (equal to monk level) spent to fuel abilities: **Flurry of Blows** (bonus action, 1 ki, two unarmed strikes), **Patient Defense** (1 ki, Dodge as a bonus action), **Step of the Wind** (1 ki, Disengage or Dash as a bonus action, jump distance doubled for the turn).
- **Unarmored Movement (2nd level):** Speed increases while unarmored, scaling with level.
- **Monastic Tradition (3rd level):** Choose a subclass (e.g., Way of the Open Hand, Way of Shadow, Way of the Four Elements) granting additional features.
- **Deflect Missiles (3rd level):** Use your reaction to reduce ranged weapon attack damage against you by 1d10 + Dexterity modifier + monk level; if reduced to 0, you can catch and optionally throw the missile.
- **Extra Attack (5th level):** Attack twice instead of once when you take the Attack action.
- **Stunning Strike (5th level):** When you hit with a melee weapon attack, spend 1 ki to force a Constitution save or the target is Stunned until the end of your next turn.

## Campaign Note

Toru's build emphasizes grappling, unarmed strikes, and an Eldritch Claw Tattoo (a magic item that makes his unarmed strikes count as magical and strengthens them, bypassing resistance to nonmagical attacks). His signature technique — a chest-caving palm strike — is best represented narratively as a heavy Martial Arts/Stunning Strike hit rather than a unique mechanic.
