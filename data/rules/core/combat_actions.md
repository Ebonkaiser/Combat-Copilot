# The Order of Combat & Actions in Combat

## Combat Sequence

1. **Determine surprise.** Anyone who doesn't notice a threat is surprised.
2. **Establish positions.**
3. **Roll initiative.** Each combatant rolls; determines turn order, highest to lowest.
4. **Take turns.** Each round, every combatant acts in initiative order. A surprised creature can't move or take an action/reaction on its first turn, and can't take a reaction until its turn ends.
5. **Begin the next round.** Initiative order repeats each round until combat ends.

A round is about 6 seconds of in-game time. On your turn you can **move a distance up to your speed** and **take one action**. You choose whether to move first or take your action first.

## Actions in Combat

- **Attack** — Make one melee or ranged attack (some features grant extra attacks as part of this action).
- **Cast a Spell** — Most spells with a casting time of "1 action."
- **Dash** — Gain extra movement equal to your speed for the turn.
- **Disengage** — Your movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks for the rest of the turn.
- **Dodge** — Until the start of your next turn, attack rolls against you have disadvantage (if you can see the attacker) and you have advantage on Dexterity saving throws. Lost if you're incapacitated or your speed drops to 0.
- **Help** — Aid another creature's ability check (giving advantage) or aid an ally's attack against a creature within 5 feet of you (that creature has advantage on the next attack roll against that target before your next turn).
- **Hide** — Make a Dexterity (Stealth) check to hide.
- **Ready** — Prepare to act in response to a trigger later in the round, using your reaction when the trigger occurs.
- **Search** — Devote your attention to finding something.
- **Use an Object** — Interact with a second object, or use a feature that requires the Use an Object action.

## Bonus Actions & Reactions

A **bonus action** can only be taken if a feature/spell/ability explicitly grants one, and only one bonus action per turn. A **reaction** is an instant response to a trigger, usable even on someone else's turn — you get one per round, regaining it at the start of your turn. The most common reaction is the **opportunity attack**.

## Opportunity Attacks

You can make one opportunity attack when a hostile creature you can see moves out of your reach. Use your reaction to make one melee attack against the provoking creature. The attack occurs right before the creature leaves your reach. You can avoid provoking it by taking the **Disengage** action, or if the movement itself doesn't take you out of the attacker's reach.

## Two-Weapon Fighting

When taking the Attack action with a light melee weapon in each hand, you can use a bonus action to make one melee attack with the off-hand weapon, provided both weapons have the light property. You don't add your ability modifier to the bonus-action damage unless it's negative.

## Grappling and Shoving

Instead of an attack, a creature can make a special melee attack to **grapple** or **shove**. Contested check: attacker's Strength (Athletics) vs. target's Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics), target's choice.

- **Grapple:** On a win, the target is Grappled. A grappled creature's speed becomes 0; it ends if the grappler is incapacitated or the target is removed from the grappler's reach.
- **Shove:** On a win, either knock the target Prone or push it 5 feet away.

## Cover

- **Half cover** (+2 AC and Dex saves)
- **Three-quarters cover** (+5 AC and Dex saves)
- **Full cover** (can't be targeted directly)

## Attacks of Multiple Kinds / Multiattack

A creature with the Multiattack feature (common among monsters and higher-level PCs via features like Extra Attack) makes multiple weapon attacks on its turn as part of the Attack action.
